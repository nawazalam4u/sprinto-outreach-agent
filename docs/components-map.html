The loops

Loop 1 · Research more: code scores each company as a range. While the 60 line sits inside it, the AI planner picks ONE tool (page, company profile, job board or news). Max 5 calls, 120 s.
Loop 2 · Rewrite: a draft that breaks any of the 16 rules, makes an unsupported claim, or scores under 7/10 is rewritten. Max 4 drafts, else HELD.
Loop 3 · Learn: when the Growth Lead edits a draft, the before/after pair guides the next drafts.
Main flow: steps 1 to 7. One bad domain never stops the batch: it gets a status and a written reason.
