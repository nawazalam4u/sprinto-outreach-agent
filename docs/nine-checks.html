The Nine Checks — Does This Deserve to Be Built?

Sprinto Cold Outreach Agent · automated prospect research and first email for Sprinto's Growth Lead · MESA C4 Term 2 · Case 02 · Nawaz Alam
The jobTurn a list of domains into qualified, cited first emails without the Growth Lead deciding anything mid-run.
The facts15–20 min per company, 8–15 a week. The ICP is SaaS, Series A–C, with audit pressure or enterprise pull.
The rules we must obeySend through email only to our own address. Use Hunter.io or a hard-coded person. Defend every decision.
Where the human staysThe ICP rules, the review of verdicts, and every send to a real prospect.

⚡ Kill Switches

Any No means: don't build it.

01Problem real?YES

15–20 min of research per company × 8–15 companies a week = 2–5 Growth Lead hours a week. Emails late in the list get worse because she is tired, not because the companies fit less.

02Workflow repeated?YES

The same four moves every week, for every new list: read the site, judge fit, find the person, write the opener. The ICP stays the same; only the domains change.

03Input available?YES

Company sites are public (robots.txt respected). Job boards have free APIs (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby). Funding and headcount come from Google Search with citations. People come from Hunter.io (25 free searches a month). No LinkedIn scraping.

📊 Sizing

Any No means: build something smaller.

04Output valuable?YES

Per company: a verdict with its score range, a named decision-maker with the reason for picking them, two pains tied to quoted evidence, and an email that passed 16 rules. She can act on it as it stands.

05Impact measurable?YES

Logged on every run: minutes per company (vs 17.5 by hand), drafts held vs sent, reviewer score. Logged by the Growth Lead in the Review tab: verdict agreement, how much she edited, and replies.

06ROI worth it?YES

About 10–20 Gemini Flash calls per company, so cents per company. Hunter, Resend and Vercel free tiers cover a 15-company week. In return, 2–5 hours a week go back into calls.

🚧 Boundary

Any No means: put a human exactly here.

07Failure risk OK?YES → own inbox only

A wrong verdict is cheap: the evidence is on screen and she can overrule it. A wrong email to a real prospect is not: it costs the brand and raises GDPR/DPDP exposure. So To: is hard-coded to her own inbox, and a draft that fails any check is held.

👤 Human placed exactly here: the Growth Lead forwards each email to the real prospect herself.
08Judgment protected?YES → human sets the ICP

The AI never sets a score. It extracts quoted facts and picks which page to read next. Code checks that each quote is real, applies the gates and computes the 100-point rubric.

👤 Human placed exactly here: she owns the ICP rules and thresholds (one file) and reviews every verdict.
09Owner clear?YES

Growth Lead: the ICP, the send decision and the review log. Builder: data sources, prompts, the 16 email rules and the kill switch. The data lives in her browser, so nobody else can see it.

👤 Human placed exactly here: one owner per decision, written down before the build.
Verdict: BUILD IT. All three kill switches and all three sizing checks are YES, so we build the full 7-step system rather than a smaller one. The boundary checks set where the human stays: the system researches, qualifies, writes and sends to the Growth Lead's own inbox. She decides what reaches a real prospect.
The Cut (what we deliberately did NOT automate): Sending to real prospects (To: is always her own inbox) · Follow-ups and replies (the L6 agent, not this build) · Scraping LinkedIn or anything behind a login · Guessing email addresses (Hunter or nothing) · Letting the AI set the score (the AI cites, code calculates).

Five Pre-Build Decisions

OutcomeAfter this system runs, the Growth Lead researches a prospect in under 2 minutes instead of 15–20, and sends at least twice as many first emails a week at an equal or better reply rate.
Baseline8–15 companies a week, 15–20 min each (2–5 hours), quality falling late in the list. Still needed: today's reply rate and meetings per 100 emails. Pull these from HubSpot or Gmail before day 1.
Workflow changeShe moves from researcher-and-writer to reviewer-and-sender. Monday: paste the domain list. Then work through "Needs you", forward the emails that passed, and log edits and replies in the Review tab.
Measurement · 90 daysDays 1–30: minutes per company, heavy-rewrite rate (target under 20%), verdict agreement (target over 80%). Days 31–60: reply rate vs the baseline. Days 61–90: meetings booked per 100 emails. All measured in her inbox and CRM, not in our logs.
Kill criterionStop if any sent email carries a claim not backed by evidence, if more than 50% of emails are heavily rewritten at day 30, if PoC accuracy falls below 60% (wrong person or bounced), or if running the system takes longer than the time it saves.
