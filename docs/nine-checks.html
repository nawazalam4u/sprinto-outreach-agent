The Nine Checks: does this deserve to be built?

Sprinto Prospect Quest · automated prospect research and first email for Sprinto's Growth Lead · numbers measured on the live build
The jobDomains in → qualified, cited first emails out. No decisions mid-run.
The facts15–20 min per company, 8–15 a week. ICP: SaaS, Series A–C, audit or enterprise pressure.
The rulesSend only to our own inbox. Hunter.io or a hard-coded person. Defend every decision.
Human staysICP rules, verdict review, and every send to a real prospect.

Kill switches

Any No → don't build it

01Problem real?YES

15–20 min per company × 8–15 a week = 2–5 hours. Late-list emails get worse from fatigue, not fit.

02Workflow repeated?YES

Same 4 moves every week: read the site, judge fit, find the person, write the opener.

03Input available?YES

Public sites (robots.txt respected), job-board APIs, Hunter.io, news. No LinkedIn scraping.

Sizing

Any No → build something smaller

04Output valuable?YES

A verdict with its score, the right person, 2 cited pains and a checked email she can send.

05Impact measurable?YES

Measured on 14 live companies: about 1 min each vs 17.5 by hand; 7 verdicts changed by research.

06ROI worth it?YES

About $0.006 per company ($0.016 with an email). Free tiers cover a week. 2–5 hours back.

Boundary

Any No → put a human exactly here

07Failure risk OK?YES → own inbox

A wrong verdict is cheap. A wrong email to a real prospect is not.

Human here: To: is hard-coded to her inbox. She forwards.
08Judgment protected?YES → she sets ICP

AI quotes facts and picks pages. Code scores, gates and checks every email.

Human here: She owns the ICP rules and reviews verdicts.
09Owner clear?YES

Growth Lead: ICP, send, review log. Builder: sources, prompts, rules, kill switch.

Human here: One owner per decision, written before the build.
Verdict: BUILD IT. All kill switches and sizing checks pass, so we build the full 7-step system. The agent researches, qualifies, writes and sends to her own inbox; she decides what reaches a real prospect.
The Cut, deliberately not automated: Sending to real prospects · Follow-ups and replies (the L6 agent) · LinkedIn or anything behind a login · Guessing email addresses · Letting the AI set the score.

Five pre-build decisions

01OutcomeResearch per prospect drops from 15–20 min to under 2, and first emails per week at least double at an equal or better reply rate.
02Baseline8–15 companies a week, 2–5 hours. Still needed: today's reply rate (pull from HubSpot or Gmail before day 1).
03Workflow changeShe moves from researcher-writer to reviewer-sender: paste the list, clear "Needs you", forward what passed.
04Measure · 90 daysDays 1–30: minutes per company, heavy rewrites under 20%, agreement over 80%. Then reply rate, then meetings per 100 emails.
05Kill criterionStop if any sent email has an unbacked claim, over 50% heavy rewrites at day 30, PoC accuracy under 60%, or it costs more time than it saves.
